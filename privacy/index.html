Loot Learning Privacy Policy

Loot Learning for iPhone and iPad.

Effective date: 2026-11-12

Loot Learning is a reading-practice app for iPhone and iPad. A child plays games, and the game pauses now and then for a short reading exercise. A parent or guardian sets the app up and chooses the reading level. This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version is that Loot Learning does not collect information about you or your child.

What stays on the device

The app keeps the following on your iPhone or iPad only:

  • Player profiles you create: a first name or nickname (up to 20 characters), an avatar, and a reading level. A "Guest" player needs no name.
  • Reading practice progress for each player, used to show the parent report inside the app.
  • Settings, such as sound options and game choices.
  • A simple activity log on the device that the app keeps for itself. It uses a random player code, not the name you typed. The app has no way to send it anywhere.

Nothing in this list is sent to us or to anyone else. There is no account, no sign-in and no server. The app works without an internet connection. Deleting the app deletes this information.

Purchases

Two premium games, The Saw and Lantern Dungeon, are unlocked by an optional auto-renewing subscription. The other games and all three reading levels are free. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). We do not see or store your payment details, Apple Account, or name.

  • Each time the app opens, it asks Apple whether a subscription is active. The app does not save a purchase record of its own for this.
  • The app does not send Apple any player name, progress or other information about your child.
  • Apple may give us summary sales and subscription reports that do not identify you. If you test a pre-release version through TestFlight, Apple may also share crash reports and basic usage numbers with us under your TestFlight settings.
  • Prices, free-trial terms and renewal terms are shown by Apple at the time of purchase. You can cancel at any time in your Apple Account settings (see our support page).

Voice and sound

Spoken words in the app are narrated either from audio files that come with the app or by your device's built-in text-to-speech voice. This happens on the device. The app does not record audio, and it does not use the microphone or camera.

No ads, no tracking, no third parties

Loot Learning has no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party software that collects data. We do not sell or share information. The app has no chat, no user-generated content and no web browsing. The only links it opens are the privacy policy and the terms, which open in your device's web browser.

Children's privacy

The app is meant to be bought and set up by a parent or guardian, and a grown-up check protects the subscription page. We do not ask a child for personal information, and we do not collect personal information from anyone through the app. Because information stays on the device, we have nothing about your child to share, correct or delete on our side. Removing a player in the app takes them off the player list, but their saved practice stays on the device; deleting the app removes everything the app saved. If you have a question about children's privacy, contact us below.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date.

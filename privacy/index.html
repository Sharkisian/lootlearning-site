Loot Learning Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 8, 2026 · Last updated: July 8, 2026

Loot Learning is an iPad reading game built for beginning and struggling readers. It is bought and set up by a parent or guardian, and used by a young reader. We built it so that learning data never leaves the iPad. This policy explains, in plain language, exactly what information the app handles and where it goes.

The short version

  • We never ask for — and never receive — anything that identifies you or your reader. No account, no sign-up, no name, no email, no birthdate, no photos, no location, no contacts, no microphone or camera.
  • All learning data stays on your iPad. Progress, answers, and practice history are stored only on the device and are deleted when you delete the app.
  • There are no ads, and nothing tracks or profiles the reader. The app contains no advertising and no analytics service that observes behavior or learning. Nothing is sold or shared for advertising. Ever.
  • The only information that leaves the device relates to your subscription purchase: Apple processes the payment, and our subscription processor (RevenueCat) receives the purchase history under a random identifier so the app knows the subscription is active. That random identifier is the single piece of transmitted data that privacy laws treat as "personal information" — the section below explains exactly how it is used and limited.

What stays on the iPad (and never leaves it)

To teach well, the app remembers things on the device only:

  • Learning progress: which reading skills have been practiced and mastered, and a review schedule.
  • Practice history: which exercises were answered, whether the first try was correct, and how long answers took. We record this so the app can pace practice correctly and so families can see real progress.
  • Session activity: when the app was opened and how long it was used.

This information is stored in the app's private storage on your iPad. It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else — the app contains no mechanism to upload it. Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete on a server, because we don't have it.

What leaves the device: subscription purchases only

Loot Learning offers an auto-renewing subscription ($12/month or $96/year) purchased through Apple's App Store, by the parent or guardian, behind a parental gate.

  • Apple processes the payment. We never see your name, payment details, or Apple Account. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat, our subscription-management provider, receives the purchase receipt so the app can confirm the subscription is active (including after you reinstall or switch iPads). This includes the purchase history for this app, a randomly generated identifier, and basic device information (such as device model and language). This random identifier is not your name, email, or anything that identifies you or your reader, and we never link it to any personal information. RevenueCat's handling of this data is described in RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • About the "Analytics" entry on our App Store privacy label: purchase history also powers our subscription business metrics (counts of purchases, renewals, and cancellations), which Apple's label taxonomy calls "Analytics." This is arithmetic about subscriptions — it is not behavioral analytics, and it never involves the reader's activity, learning data, or identity.

That is the complete list. The app makes no other network connections.

Children's privacy (COPPA notice)

Loot Learning is used by young readers, so we hold ourselves to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws.

  • We never ask children for personal information. The app has no field, prompt, or feature that requests a name, age, birthdate, contact information, photos, voice, or location from the reader, and it has no chat, sharing, or social features.
  • No learning or behavioral data about the reader ever leaves the device.
  • The app has no advertising of any kind, and no analytics service that observes the reader's behavior. (Our subscription processor counts purchases and renewals, as described above — that is the only "analytics" involved, and it contains nothing about the reader.)
  • Persistent identifier notice (support for internal operations): one persistent identifier is collected — the random subscription ID described above — and under COPPA a persistent identifier counts as personal information. We collect it under COPPA's "support for internal operations" exception, and it is used solely for those internal operations — specifically: processing and verifying the subscription purchased by the parent, restoring that purchase across reinstalls and devices, and preventing payment fraud. It is used for no other purpose: not for behavioral advertising, not for profiling, and not to contact anyone. We enforce this by design — the app integrates no advertising or profiling services, and our subscription processor is contractually limited to providing subscription services.
  • Purchases are made by the parent or guardian through their own Apple Account, behind an in-app parental gate.

Because the only personal information collected is that persistent identifier, used strictly to support internal operations, COPPA does not require verifiable parental consent for the app's operation. If we ever change what the app collects, we will update this policy first and, where the law requires it, obtain verifiable parental consent before any change takes effect.

Your choices and rights

  • See or delete learning data: it's all on your iPad. Deleting the app deletes it. We hold nothing.
  • Manage or cancel the subscription: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your device, or via the App Store. Cancelling stops future renewals; refunds are handled by Apple through reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Questions or concerns: email us at evan.sarkisian@gmail.com. We're a small, independent developer and we answer directly. If you believe a child's personal information has somehow been collected, contact us and we will investigate and delete it.

Beta versions (TestFlight)

If you use a pre-release version through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash logs and basic usage statistics with us if you opt in, as described in TestFlight's own terms. The app itself behaves as described above in beta too.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behavior changes (for example, an optional progress-sharing feature for parents), we will update this policy before shipping the change, update the "last updated" date, and describe the change plainly at the top of this page.

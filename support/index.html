Loot Learning Support

Help with the iPad reading game.

Loot Learning is made by a small, independent developer — questions go straight to a person, not a ticket queue. Email evan.sarkisian@gmail.com and we'll get back to you quickly.

Common questions

  • Manage or cancel your subscription: on your iPad, open Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Loot Learning. You can also manage it in the App Store app. Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep access until the end of the paid period.
  • Restore a purchase on a new or reinstalled iPad: open the app, go to the parent area, and tap "Restore Purchases." Make sure the iPad is signed into the same Apple Account that bought the subscription.
  • Refunds: purchases are processed by Apple, so refund requests go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Delete your reader's data: everything lives on your iPad — deleting the app deletes all learning data. Nothing is stored on a server. Details in our Privacy Policy.
  • Found a bug, or a level that isn't teaching well? Please tell us — include what happened and your reader's level ("Sounds & Letters" or "Reading & Spelling Words") if you can. Real family reports drive what gets fixed first.

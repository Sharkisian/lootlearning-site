Loot Learning Support

Help with the reading-practice app for iPhone and iPad.

Loot Learning is a reading-practice app for iPhone and iPad. A child plays a real game, the game pauses at natural breaks for a short reading exercise, and play carries on afterward. A parent or guardian picks the reading level. The app works offline and needs no account.

The games

  • The Saw (the main game) and Lantern Dungeon are the premium games. They need a subscription.
  • Brick Breaker and Bubble Shooter are free.
  • All three reading levels, player profiles, the parent report and the settings are free.

If a subscription ends, nothing is deleted. The premium games simply lock again.

Reading levels

Pick the level by what your child can do now, not by age. You can change it later.

  • Sounds & Letters: letter sounds, sounds in words, rhyming, first words, and letter tracing.
  • Reading & Spelling Words: sight words, blends, digraphs, and longer words.
  • Longer Words & Spelling Patterns: silent-e, bossy-r, and two-syllable words.

To change a child's level: open PARENTS on the Home screen and tap Player settings (or tap the player button at the top of Home) to reach Who's playing?. Tap the pencil on the player's card, pick a Reading level, then tap Save. The Saw's Game challenge (Relaxed, Standard, Challenge) is separate from the reading level; it sits next to Play on The Saw's page and in The Saw · setup.

Subscription

Unlock the premium games: tap Play on The Saw or Lantern Dungeon, answer the grown-up question, and choose a plan. Apple shows the price and any free trial before you confirm.

Restore a purchase: on a new device, or after reinstalling, tap Parents · Subscription & privacy on Home (or Play on a premium game), answer the grown-up question, then tap Restore Purchases. The device must be signed in to the Apple Account that bought the subscription.

Cancel: open the iPhone or iPad Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Loot Learning and tap Cancel. You can also use Manage Subscription in the app's subscription page while it is active. Cancelling stops renewal; access continues until the paid period ends. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Refunds: Apple handles purchases and refunds. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

Sound and voices

Reading practice needs sound. Most words and sentences use recordings that come with the app; when one is missing, the app uses the device's English voice. If the app says "Learning voice isn't ready", add or download an English voice and then tap Retry voice:

  1. Open Settings on the iPhone or iPad.
  2. Go to Accessibility, then Spoken Content, then Voices.
  3. Choose English, pick a voice, and download it.
  4. Return to Loot Learning and tap Retry voice.

(Menu names can vary a little by iOS version.) The speaker button on Home opens the sound settings, where you can switch reading questions to Visual only, change the reading volume, turn game sounds off, or turn on calm effects.

Your data

Everything the app keeps (players, progress, settings) is stored on your device. Nothing is sent to a server. Deleting the app deletes it. See the Privacy Policy.

Contact

Email hello@lootlearning.com. For a bug report, please include your device (iPhone or iPad model), iOS version, the game, and the reading level.

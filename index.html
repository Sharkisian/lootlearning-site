Loot Learning

Real games with short reading practice built in, for iPhone and iPad.

Loot Learning is a set of real games where play pauses at natural breaks for a short structured-literacy reading exercise, then carries on. It is for beginning readers, including children with dyslexia. A parent or guardian sets it up and picks the reading level. It works offline and needs no account.

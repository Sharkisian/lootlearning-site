Loot Learning

The iPad game that makes reading practice something kids ask for.

Loot Learning is a real game — glowing enemies, swords, evolving creatures — where progress runs on short bursts of structured-literacy reading practice. Built for beginning and struggling readers, including kids with dyslexia. Bought and set up by a parent; played (and asked for) by the kid.
